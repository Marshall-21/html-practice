Oppenheimer

Written, produced and directed by the great Christopher Nolan, this was one of the most important flms of 2023.

Without the proper contextual knowledge, the 3-hour plus runtime may feel rather boring.

But after I updated myself on the film's subject matter, I understood why it ultimately won a number of Oscars.

The film follows the life of J. Robert Oppenheimer, a physicist who played a key role in the development of the atomic bomb during World War II.

Nolan's signature style is on full display, with complex storytelling, stunning visuals, and a mix of real-life events and fictional storytelling.

The performances are top-notch, with standout roles from Cillian Murphy, Emily Blunt, and Matt Damon.

Overall, "Oppenheimer" is a must-watch for any film enthusiast.
