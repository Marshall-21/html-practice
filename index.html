    Given the infamous Tate murders, l wondered what direction Tarantino would take.

    But that ending was one of the most satisfying I've ever seen.

  2. Goodfellas

    This has to got to go down as one of the greatest crime dramas ever to be released.

    Martin Scorsese decided to show the world the reality of the mafia; good and bad.

    He strips away the romanticization of gangsters and shows their vile nature.

    And the cast was just as impressive boasting the likes of De Niro,Ray Liotta and Joe Pesci.

    The two-hour plus runtime feels rather short as Scorsese does not let up as he takes you on a crazy ride throughout.

    Let me say no more and let you see for yourself.
