Goodfellas

This has to go down as one of the greatest crime dramas ever to be released.

Martin Scorsese decided to show the world the reality of the mafia; good and bad.

He strips away the romanticization of gangsters and shows their vile nature.

The cast was just as impressive, boasting the likes of De Niro, Ray Liotta, and Joe Pesci.

The two-hour plus runtime feels rather short as Scorsese does not let up, taking you on a crazy ride throughout.

Let me say no more and let you see for yourself.

Released in 1990, this film is a masterpiece from the well-acclaimed Martin Scorsese.

The film is set in the world of organized crime, following the life of Henry Hill and his associates.

Scorsese's signature style is on full display, with sharp dialogue, dark humor, and a mix of real-life events and fictional storytelling.

The performances are top-notch, with standout roles from Ray Liotta, Robert De Niro, and Joe Pesci.

Overall, "Goodfellas" is a must-watch for any film enthusiast.
