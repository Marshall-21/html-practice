Once Upon A Time In Hollywood

Released in 2019, this film is another masterpiece from the well-acclaimed Quentin Tarantino.

The film is set in 1969 Los Angeles, a time of great change and upheaval in the city.

Given the infamous Tate murders, I wondered what direction Tarantino would take with the story.

But that ending was one of the most satisfying I've ever seen.

The film follows the lives of several characters, including a fading actor and his stunt double, as they navigate the changing landscape of Hollywood.

Tarantino's signature style is on full display, with sharp dialogue, dark humor, and a mix of real-life events and fictional storytelling.

The performances are top-notch, with standout roles from Leonardo DiCaprio, Brad Pitt, and Margot Robbie.

Overall, "Once Upon A Time In Hollywood" is a must-watch for any film enthusiast.
